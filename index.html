AutoTube

AutoTube is a personal production tool for a documentary YouTube channel. The channel owner runs it on their own computer to prepare finished videos and upload them to the channel the owner controls.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads the owner's videos and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and release times.
  • Reads the status of those videos and the channel they belong to.
  • Posts a comment on the owner's own videos when asked or switched on by the owner.

It is used by one person, for one channel. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's YouTube account.
